Website Design Checklist 2024 — 25 Things Every Business Website Must Have

Before you launch your new website — or audit your existing one — run through this interactive 25-point checklist used by professional web designers to ensure nothing critical is missing. Tick each item as you go and watch your progress.

Checklist Categories
  • ADesign & Branding (5 items)
  • BContent & Messaging (5 items)
  • CTechnical & Performance (5 items)
  • DSEO Foundations (5 items)
  • EConversion & Lead Generation (5 items)

Whether you're launching a brand-new website or reviewing an existing one, this checklist covers the 25 elements that professional web designers verify before any website goes live. Miss even 3–4 of these and your website is likely underperforming significantly — costing you leads and credibility every day.

0 of 25 items checked

A — Design & Branding

First impressions are formed in milliseconds. These 5 design elements determine whether visitors instantly trust and recognise your brand.

1. Consistent brand colours and fonts throughout

Your exact brand colour palette and typography are used consistently on every page — no off-brand colours, no inconsistent fonts between pages.

2. High-quality logo (SVG or 2x PNG)

Your logo displays crisp and sharp on all screen sizes and retina displays — not blurry or pixelated on any device.

3. Professional, on-brand imagery

All photos and illustrations are high quality and aligned with your brand. No blurry stock photos that undermine credibility with visitors.

4. Consistent spacing and alignment

Elements are evenly spaced, properly aligned, and visually balanced on all pages — nothing feels cramped, overflowing, or misaligned.

5. Favicon set up

A small branded icon appears in browser tabs, bookmarks, and on mobile home screens when users save your website to their device.

B — Content & Messaging

The content on your website either builds confidence or destroys it. These 5 items ensure your messaging is clear, credible, and compelling.

6. Clear value proposition on the homepage

Within 5 seconds of landing, visitors understand exactly what you do, who you help, and why they should choose you over every competitor.

7. Zero placeholder text anywhere

Absolutely no "Lorem ipsum," "Your text here," or filler content anywhere on the live website. Every word is intentional and real.

8. Copy has been proofread end-to-end

All text has been proofread for spelling, grammar, and clarity. Errors in copy instantly damage business credibility and trust.

9. About page with genuine information

A real, humanising about page that tells your story, introduces your team (where relevant), and builds trust with potential customers visiting for the first time.

10. Current and correct contact information

Your phone number, email address, and physical address (if applicable) are up-to-date, correct, and easy to find on every page of your website.

Quick Test: Have someone completely unfamiliar with your business visit your homepage for the first time. After just 10 seconds, ask them: what does this business do and who do they serve? If they struggle to answer clearly, your messaging needs significant work before launch.

C — Technical & Performance

Technical issues silently kill good websites. These 5 checks ensure your website actually works the way it should for all visitors.

11. SSL certificate active — HTTPS in address bar

The padlock icon shows in the browser bar. Without SSL, browsers display "Not Secure" warnings and Google ranks you lower. This is non-negotiable in 2024.

12. Fully responsive on mobile and tablet

Tested and verified on iPhone, Android, and multiple tablet sizes. Nothing is broken, cut off, or difficult to interact with on any screen size.

13. Page load time under 3 seconds

Verified using Google PageSpeed Insights. Both mobile and desktop scores are measured and improved. Anything over 3 seconds costs you visitors and rankings.

14. No broken links or 404 errors

Every internal and external link on your website leads to a working page. Broken links damage both user experience and your SEO performance significantly.

15. Cross-browser compatible

Your website displays and functions correctly in Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge — the four most commonly used browsers by Indian internet users.

D — SEO Foundations

Without these 5 basics, even a beautifully designed website will struggle to rank on Google and attract organic traffic.

16. Unique title tags on every page

Every page has a distinct, keyword-relevant title tag between 50–60 characters. No two pages share the same title. See our guide on common SEO mistakes to understand why this matters.

17. Meta descriptions written for every page

Compelling, unique meta descriptions of 140–160 characters that accurately describe each page and encourage real people to click from Google search results.

18. Correct H1 structure — one per page

Each page has exactly one H1 containing the primary keyword, followed by a logical H2 and H3 hierarchy that structures all the content on the page.

19. XML sitemap submitted to Google

An up-to-date XML sitemap is submitted via Google Search Console so all your pages are properly discovered and indexed by Google without delay.

20. Google Analytics and Search Console connected

Both tools are properly set up and verified — enabling you to track traffic, understand rankings, and make data-informed decisions from your very first day live.

E — Conversion & Lead Generation

These 5 items are what separate a beautiful-but-useless website from one that actually generates leads and revenue for your business.

21. Clear call-to-action on every key page

Every page guides visitors toward a specific next step — whether that's calling you, filling in a form, making a purchase, or booking a free consultation.

22. Contact form tested and confirmed working

Your contact form has been submitted and the delivery to your email verified. Forms that silently fail are more common than you think — and cost you every enquiry.

23. Phone number is click-to-call on mobile

On smartphones, your phone number is a clickable tel: link that instantly initiates a call — reducing friction for the majority of Indian users browsing on mobile.

24. Social proof is prominently visible

Client testimonials, Google reviews, case studies, industry certifications, or trust badges are displayed prominently to build confidence with first-time visitors.

25. Privacy policy page exists and is linked

A privacy policy is legally required if you collect any visitor data (contact forms, analytics, cookies) and is expected by customers who take their data privacy seriously.
