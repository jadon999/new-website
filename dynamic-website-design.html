What Is a Dynamic Website?

The Intelligent, Flexible
Website Architecture

A dynamic website connects to a database and generates page content in real time — displaying information that changes based on who's viewing it, what they've searched for, or what's been updated in the admin panel. This makes dynamic websites ideal for any business with complex, frequently changing, or user-specific content needs.


From WordPress-powered business websites to fully custom web applications, dynamic websites give your business the tools to serve content, process transactions, manage users, and deliver personalised experiences at scale — all through one unified platform you control.

A dynamic website generates content on-the-fly from a database — displaying personalised, frequently updated, or user-specific content. Unlike static websites, dynamic sites support user accounts, blogs, ecommerce, booking systems, admin panels, and content that adapts based on who is viewing the page and what they're looking for.
You need a dynamic website when your business requires regularly updated content (blogs, news), user accounts or memberships, ecommerce functionality, booking or appointment systems, a searchable product catalogue, or a custom admin panel for managing business data. For most businesses that only need a stable online presence, a static website is more efficient.
