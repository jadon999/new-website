The Mobile Reality

Why Responsive Design
Matters for Your Business

India is a mobile-first nation. If your website doesn't perform perfectly on smartphones, you are invisible to the majority of your potential customers — and penalised by Google.

📱

75%+ Mobile Traffic in India

The majority of your website visitors are using smartphones. A non-responsive site delivers a broken experience that drives them straight to your competitors.

🔍

Google's Mobile-First Index

Google crawls and ranks the mobile version of websites first. A poor mobile experience directly hurts your ability to rank in search results — even for desktop queries.

📉

High Bounce Rate Without It

Users who land on a non-responsive website leave within seconds. High bounce rates signal to Google that your website isn't providing value — further damaging rankings.

💰

Lost Revenue Every Day

Every visitor who leaves your site because of a poor mobile experience is a lost sale. A responsive design converts mobile visitors just as effectively as desktop users.
